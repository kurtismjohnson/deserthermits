Writing About

The name "Desert Hermits" has been with me for a while now, but here it points to how writing is deeply personal, but can still reach far beyond yourself. It's something solitary that carries a message far and wide. Maybe others read the written words directly, or maybe writing for yourself, looking internally, makes you more available to those around you. I think about the prayer of the monastics: a deeply personal endeavor that still serves a community, or perhaps even the entire world. There. Does that sound pretentious enough?

I am a writer based in Atlanta, Georgia. Feel free to say hello at hi@kurtismjohnson.me or over at @deserthermits.
