Writing About

The name "Desert Hermits" has been with me for a while now, but here it points to how writing is profoundly personal yet can still reach far beyond yourself. It's a solitary act that carries a message far and wide. Maybe the written words are read directly by others, or perhaps writing for yourself, the intentional introspection, allows you to be more available to those around you. I think about the prayer of the monastics: a deeply personal endeavor that still serves a community, or even the entire world. There. Does that sound pretentious enough?

I am a writer based in Atlanta, Georgia. Feel free to say hello at hi@kurtismjohnson.me or over at @deserthermits.
